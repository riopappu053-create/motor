QUESTIONS FROM THE BENCH.

ASK AN ENGINEER
Q.01

DO YOU SUPPLY THE DONOR BIKE?

We source donors on request through our network of collectors, but most clients supply their own. We assess any donor free of charge before a build slot is confirmed.

Q.02

ARE BUILDS STREET LEGAL?

Every commission ships with lighting, silencing and emissions hardware compliant for road registration, plus documentation for your local inspection authority.

Q.03

HOW IS PAYMENT STRUCTURED?

A 30% deposit reserves your bench slot, 40% is invoiced at the fabrication milestone, and the balance is due on final dyno sign-off before collection or crating.

Q.04

DO YOU SHIP INTERNATIONALLY?

Yes. We crate in marine-grade plywood with desiccant and foam blocking, and coordinate air or sea freight with full customs documentation prepared in-house.

Q.05

CAN I WATCH THE BUILD PROGRESS?

Every client receives a private photo log updated weekly, plus two scheduled workshop visits — one at dry-fit and one at the dyno session.

Q.06

WHAT WARRANTY IS INCLUDED?

All fabrication and engine work carries a 24-month workmanship warranty. Electrical systems and coatings are covered for 12 months from collection date.
